# Outreach Message: Renewal Pain Interview

Last updated: 2026-08-06
Status: Active outreach artifact
Task ID: TASK-006

## Purpose

This is the default outreach message for recruiting target buyers into the first renewal-copilot validation sprint.

## Short Version

Hi [Name] — I’m doing focused research on how mid-market teams handle SaaS and AI renewals when there isn’t a dedicated procurement function.

I’m looking to speak with finance, IT, or operations owners who deal with vendor renewals, pricing pressure, or late notice windows. The goal is to understand what actually goes wrong today and whether a benchmark-backed decision brief would be useful before renewal decisions get locked in.

This would be a 20-minute conversation. If it’s relevant, I’d also ask whether you’d be open to walking through one recent or upcoming renewal at a high level.

Would you be open to it?

## Longer Version

Hi [Name],

I’m researching how mid-market companies manage SaaS and AI renewals when procurement is handled part-time by finance, IT, or operations rather than by a dedicated sourcing team.

I’m specifically trying to learn:

- what usually goes wrong during renewals
- how teams decide whether to renew, renegotiate, downgrade, or replace a vendor
- whether benchmark-backed decision guidance would actually change behavior

I’m not selling anything in this conversation.

I’m looking for a 20-minute interview with someone who has touched real software or AI renewals recently. If the conversation is relevant, I may also ask whether you’d be open to walking through one live or recent renewal at a high level so I can understand the decision process more concretely.

If this is close to your workflow, would you be open to a short conversation?

## Target Personas

- VP Finance
- Head of Operations
- IT or systems owner
- procurement-adjacent operator in a mid-market company

## Screening Reminder

Prioritize companies that:

- have `150` to `1,000` employees
- have meaningful SaaS or AI spend
- do not have a fully staffed procurement team
- have at least `3` meaningful renewals in the next `120` days
