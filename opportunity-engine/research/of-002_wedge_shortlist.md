## Purpose

This artifact narrows `OF-002 Decision and Foresight Infrastructure` into a short list of buildable wedges.

Its job is to give Atlas one recommended build candidate by default while preserving the strongest backup lanes.

## Recommended Candidate

### Benchmark-backed SaaS and AI renewal decision copilot

- Why this is first:
  - strongest link between decision quality and measurable ROI
  - recurring workflow with clear timing pressure
  - can start as decision intelligence before expanding into execution automation
  - fits the current evidence that AI and software spend is volatile while procurement AI is still pre-scale
- What makes it different from a generic procurement platform:
  - starts with SaaS and AI renewals, not all categories
  - focuses on decision prep, benchmark context, and approval-ready recommendations
  - targets companies without a full procurement stack or team

## Backup Candidate

### Product roadmap decision copilot

- Why it stays on the board:
  - decision pain is real
  - PM workflows are high leverage
  - it stays close to the Atlas thesis of better judgment before action
- Why it is not first:
  - incumbents already occupy the prioritization workflow
  - the market is more feature-complete than it first appears

## What To Validate Next

Atlas should now validate the recommended wedge with direct evidence:

1. Do mid-market teams actually feel this pain acutely enough to change behavior?
2. Is benchmark-backed renewal prep enough to trigger willingness to pay?
3. Can Atlas access or build benchmark intelligence that is differentiated enough to defend?
4. Is the wedge strong enough without becoming a services-heavy procurement consultancy?
