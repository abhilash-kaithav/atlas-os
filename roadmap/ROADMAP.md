# Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-08-06
Status: Active

## Current Phase

Atlas has completed the first Opportunity Family taxonomy pass. The immediate phase is to score and triage those approved families before deeper research begins.

The goal is still not to judge all 700 ideas one by one. The goal is to compare larger strategic families first so Atlas can decide where deeper research and validation effort should concentrate.

## Current Priorities

1. Execute `TASK-003` Opportunity Family Triage from the approved Opportunity Family layer.
2. Score each family with an explicit rubric and short rationale.
3. Preserve traceability from every family score back through the Opportunity Family and Value Pattern maps to the concept inventory.

## Next Milestones

1. Score or triage the approved Opportunity Families instead of raw concepts.
2. Research the highest-potential families before committing to MVP work.
3. Define one-page family memos for the strongest wedges.
4. Validate the best family-level wedges with direct evidence.
5. Define venture candidates only after family-level research supports them.

## Upcoming Triage Work

Opportunity Family Triage should follow these rules:

1. Start from the approved Opportunity Family layer, not direct concept-level intuition.
2. Score families with explicit, reusable criteria rather than vibe-based preference.
3. Keep representative concepts, family evidence gaps, and uncertainty visible for every score.
4. Compare families before drilling into specific venture ideas.
5. Keep the raw concept, Value Pattern, and Opportunity Family layers all traceable.

## Major Decisions Already Made

1. The repository is the source of truth.
2. Broad generation precedes prioritization.
3. Recommendations should be concise and best-answer-first.
4. Evidence is required before strategy changes.
5. The raw and curated concept layers remain separate.
6. Atlas operating model v1.0 governs execution workflow.
7. Atlas reasoning model v1.0 governs methodology.
8. Atlas Value Pattern Taxonomy v1.0 is the active first discovery layer above the concept schema.
9. Atlas Opportunity Family Taxonomy v1.0 is the active second discovery layer above Value Patterns.
