# Live Renewal Teardown Request

Last updated: 2026-08-06
Status: Active outreach artifact
Task ID: TASK-006

## Purpose

This is the default follow-up request for securing a live or recent renewal teardown after a successful interview.

## Request Copy

Thanks again for the conversation.

You mentioned that [Vendor / Renewal Type] is either coming up soon or was handled recently, and that it reflects the kind of decision process we discussed.

Would you be open to a guided teardown of that renewal?

I’m not looking for sensitive data to be stored permanently, and I don’t need anything beyond what is necessary to understand the workflow and the decision itself.

The minimum useful package is:

- contract or order form
- recent spend or invoice history
- renewal date and notice terms
- basic usage or seat counts if available

The goal is to produce a decision brief that shows:

- pricing and leverage questions
- possible downgrade or consolidation signals
- renewal timing risks
- what information was missing at decision time

If you’re open to it, we can redact sensitive details and keep the exercise tightly scoped to the decision process.

## Acceptance Standard

A teardown candidate is qualified when the buyer can provide:

- a real or recent renewal
- enough context to reconstruct the decision
- at least part of the minimum data package

## Non-Repository Rule

Do not commit sensitive customer documents or raw confidential details to the repository.

Only store anonymized notes and synthesized findings.
