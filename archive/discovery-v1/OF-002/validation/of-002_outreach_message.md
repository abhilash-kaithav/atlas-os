# Outreach Message: Renewal Pain Interview

Last updated: 2026-08-07
Status: Active outreach artifact
Task ID: TASK-007

## Purpose

This is the active outreach message set for recruiting target buyers into the first renewal-copilot validation sprint.

Use the variant that matches the access path.

## Core Framing

Keep the explanation simple:

- this is founder-led research
- this is not a sales call
- the topic is SaaS and AI renewals in mid-market companies without a full procurement team
- the ask is a `20`-minute conversation
- a teardown request only comes later if the interview is relevant

## Warm Intro Blurb

Use this when asking a mutual contact to forward you:

`I'm researching how mid-market teams handle SaaS and AI renewals when there isn't a dedicated procurement function. I'm trying to speak with finance, IT, or ops leaders who see renewal pain directly. Would you be open to introducing me to one or two people who fit that profile? This is research, not a sales pitch, and the ask is only a 20-minute conversation.`

## Warm Direct Message

Use this when you already know the person:

Hi [Name] — I’m doing founder-led research on how mid-market companies handle SaaS and AI renewals when procurement is handled part-time by finance, IT, or operations rather than by a dedicated sourcing team.

I’m not selling anything in this conversation. I’m trying to understand how renewal decisions actually happen, where teams lose leverage, and whether benchmark-backed decision support would materially help before renewal deadlines lock the outcome.

Would you be open to a `20`-minute conversation? If it’s relevant, I may later ask whether you’d be willing to walk through one recent or upcoming renewal at a high level.

## Short Version

Would you be open to it?

## Longer Version

Hi [Name],

I’m doing founder-led research on how mid-market companies manage SaaS and AI renewals when procurement is handled part-time by finance, IT, or operations rather than by a dedicated sourcing team.

I’m specifically trying to learn:

- what usually goes wrong during renewals
- how teams decide whether to renew, renegotiate, downgrade, or replace a vendor
- whether benchmark-backed decision guidance would actually change behavior

I’m not selling anything in this conversation.

I’m looking for a 20-minute interview with someone who has touched real software or AI renewals recently. If the conversation is relevant, I may also ask whether you’d be open to walking through one live or recent renewal at a high level so I can understand the decision process more concretely.

If this is close to your workflow, would you be open to a short conversation?

## Follow-Up Version

Hi [Name] — following up in case this is relevant.

I’m still looking to speak with operators who have touched real SaaS or AI renewals without the support of a large procurement team. The ask is just a `20`-minute research conversation, and I’m happy to work around your schedule.

## Referral Ask After A Good Interview

This conversation was very helpful. Based on what you’ve seen, is there one or two other finance, IT, or ops leaders you’d recommend I speak with who deal with similar renewal decisions?

I’m specifically looking for teams without a large procurement function, where the renewal tradeoff really sits with an operator.

## If Someone Asks "What Exactly Are You Building?"

Use this:

`I have a hypothesis around benchmark-backed renewal decision support, but right now I'm not trying to sell a finished product. I'm trying to understand the workflow, pain points, and decision economics well enough to know whether the problem is important and repeated enough to deserve building.`

## Target Personas

- VP Finance
- Head of Operations
- IT or systems owner
- procurement-adjacent operator in a mid-market company

## Screening Reminder

Prioritize companies that:

- have `150` to `1,000` employees
- have meaningful SaaS or AI spend
- do not have a fully staffed procurement team
- have at least `3` meaningful renewals in the next `120` days
