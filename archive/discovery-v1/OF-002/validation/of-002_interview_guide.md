# Interview Guide: SaaS and AI Renewal Decision Copilot

Last updated: 2026-08-06
Status: Active interview guide
Task ID: TASK-005

## Goal

Learn whether the target buyer experiences enough pain around SaaS and AI renewals to change behavior and pay for a decision system.

## Target Interviewees

- VP Finance
- Head of Operations
- IT or systems owner
- procurement-adjacent operator in a mid-market company without a large procurement team

## Screening Questions

Use these before scheduling:

1. How many employees does the company have?
2. Who owns software and AI renewals today?
3. How many meaningful software or AI renewals are expected in the next `120` days?
4. Is there a dedicated procurement team, or is this handled by finance, IT, or operations?
5. Would the team be able to discuss a recent renewal decision in detail?

## Interview Structure

### 1. Current workflow

- Walk me through the last meaningful SaaS or AI renewal your team handled.
- Who was involved?
- What information did you have at the start?
- What was confusing or uncertain?

### 2. Pain and consequences

- What usually goes wrong in these renewals?
- Where do you feel least confident?
- Have you overpaid, renewed too late, or missed a consolidation opportunity in the last year?
- How expensive was that mistake in time, money, or risk?

### 3. Existing solutions

- What tools, brokers, or advisors do you use today?
- What do they do well?
- What is still missing?
- If you already have procurement support, why is the problem not fully solved?

### 4. Benchmark value

- If you had a decision brief showing fair-price range, savings opportunity, contract risk, and recommended negotiation posture, what would that change?
- Which part of that brief would matter most?
- What would make you distrust it?

### 5. Buying motion

- Who would have to approve this kind of tool?
- Would this come from a finance budget, IT budget, operations budget, or something else?
- Is this a problem you would pay to solve as software, or only as a service?

### 6. Live teardown ask

- Do you have one live or recent renewal you would be willing to walk through with us?
- Could you share the minimum data package:
  - contract or order form
  - recent spend or invoice history
  - renewal date and notice terms
  - basic usage or seat counts if available

## What To Listen For

Strong validation signals:

- repeated mention of late renewals, weak leverage, or unclear pricing
- budget owner is obvious
- buyer says a benchmark-backed brief would change action
- willingness to share a live renewal
- openness to a software-first or hybrid product

Weak validation signals:

- problem sounds infrequent or mostly annoying rather than expensive
- incumbent broker or procurement tool already solves it
- buyer wants full-service negotiation only
- no one can share real data

## Interview Close

End every call by asking:

1. Would you want to see a sample decision brief for one upcoming renewal?
2. If yes, which renewal should we look at first?
3. Who else on your team would need to see this to take it seriously?
