# O-002 Behavioral Primitives

Last updated: 2026-08-03
Status: Active observation
Category: Behavior design

## Observation

Across independent behavior-first explorations, the strongest concepts appear to be behavioral primitives (e.g., decision drafts, learning loops, trust trials, opportunity queues) that can be instantiated across many industries rather than tied to a single vertical.

## What Was Observed

- High-signal ideas repeatedly described a reusable shift in human behavior rather than a market-specific workflow.
- `Decision Drafts` can apply to hiring, investing, creative work, and operations without changing the core behavior.
- `Learning Loops`, `Trust Trials`, and `Opportunity Queues` similarly act as portable patterns that can be adapted across contexts.
- The appeal of these concepts seems to come from the repeatable behavioral structure, not from deep dependence on one industry.

## Boundaries

- This note records a recurring pattern, not a conclusion that vertical-specific opportunities are weak.
- Some vertical concepts may still win if urgency, distribution, or proprietary access outweigh cross-industry portability.
- The examples here are early pattern signals, not proof that every behavioral primitive is commercially strong.

## Review Implication

When evaluating future opportunities, explicitly ask whether the concept is a reusable behavioral primitive that can travel across industries or whether it depends on one vertical's specific workflow.
