# Principles

Last updated: 2026-08-03
Status: Active

This directory is reserved for operating principles that have been earned through repeated evidence rather than declared from intuition.

## Admission Rule

A principle may be added here only when:

1. a hypothesis has repeated support across multiple independent evidence points or operating cycles
2. at least one serious attempt has been made to disprove it
3. the resulting rule is stable enough to guide future work by default

## What Belongs Here

- durable operating truths
- decision rules that have survived contradiction checks

## What Does Not Belong Here

- raw observations
- early explanations
- untested intuitions

Until those thresholds are met, keep the work in `knowledge/observations/` or `knowledge/hypotheses/`.
