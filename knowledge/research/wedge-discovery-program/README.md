## Purpose

This folder is the canonical home of the Atlas wedge-discovery program.

The program's job is to narrow the Top 50 industries into a durable portfolio of atomic wedges with explicit `GREEN`, `KILL`, or `MERGE` outcomes.

It does not choose a company.

It does not force every industry to yield a wedge.

It preserves evidence and reduces search space.
