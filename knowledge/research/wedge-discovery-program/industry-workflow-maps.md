# Industry Workflow Maps

Last updated: 2026-08-15
Status: Phase 0 and Phase 0B complete for the Top 50

## Purpose

This file is the canonical Phase 0 layer for the frozen Atlas Research Program v1.0 methodology.

Each operating-system map below is the reusable end-to-end workflow map for every industry in that operating system. Each section explicitly includes operational, financial, compliance, recovery, cross-functional handoff, and external-party interactions so later wedge work does not overfit to the original Top 5 workflows.

## Asset Utilization and Lease Management

- Applies to: `Housing (1)`, `Other real estate (5)`, `Rental and leasing services and lessors of intangible assets (23)`
- Lifecycle map: demand generation and asset sourcing -> qualification and underwriting -> lease, reservation, or contract setup -> occupancy, dispatch, or asset deployment -> maintenance and service execution -> billing, cash application, or asset recovery -> turnover, return, or make-ready -> compliance, owner, or investor reporting -> renewal, remarketing, or closeout
- Industry anchors: housing centers on lease acquisition, rent cash application, and unit turns; other real estate adds tenant billing, CAM-style reconciliation, and investor reporting; rental and leasing emphasizes asset setup, checkout, return inspection, and recovery
- Phase 0B completeness: operational `yes`; financial `yes`; compliance `yes`; recovery `yes`; cross-functional handoffs `yes`; external-party interactions `yes`
