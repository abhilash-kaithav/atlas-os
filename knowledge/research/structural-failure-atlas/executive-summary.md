# Phase 3 Executive Summary

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Approved Phase 3 summary

## Headline

Atlas now has an evidence-backed Structural Failure Atlas built from the Phase 1 normalized census and the Phase 2 workflow library. The corpus shows that recurring failure is driven less by missing point software than by repeated breakdowns in context capture, judgment, exception handling, reconciliation, coordination, and adaptation to reality.

## Most Recurring Failures

- `SF-03` Decision Context Escapes the Record: 174 workflows, 21 operating systems, 50 industries, dominant root cause `Legacy Architecture`.
- `SF-04` Human Judgment Under Incomplete Information: 116 workflows, 21 operating systems, 50 industries, dominant root cause `Technical`.
- `SF-01` Exception-Path Breakdown: 77 workflows, 20 operating systems, 48 industries, dominant root cause `Legacy Architecture`.
- `SF-06` Plan vs. Reality Divergence: 53 workflows, 13 operating systems, 35 industries, dominant root cause `Legacy Architecture`.
- `SF-02` Cross-System Reconciliation: 42 workflows, 17 operating systems, 35 industries, dominant root cause `Legacy Architecture`.

## Interpretation

The two most universal failures are `SF-03` Decision Context Escapes the Record and `SF-04` Human Judgment Under Incomplete Information. Together they show that the system of record rarely contains the full narrative or tradeoff logic required to advance work.
`SF-01` Exception-Path Breakdown, `SF-06` Plan vs. Reality Divergence, and `SF-05` Handoff and Approval Latency explain why workflows that look software-covered still leak money and time once real conditions deviate from the nominal path.
`SF-02` Cross-System Reconciliation, `SF-07` Compliance and Evidence Burden, and `SF-08` Multi-Party Trust and Dependency Gaps are most concentrated where financial accuracy, regulated proof, or counterparty coordination matter more than local task completion.

## Root-Cause Pattern

Across selected failure assignments in the atlas, the most common Phase 2 root causes remain Legacy Architecture (217); Technical (125); Regulatory (114); Organizational (57); Behavioral (34); Economic (12). Legacy architecture dominates overall, but technical, regulatory, organizational, and behavioral causes all persist depending on the failure family.

## Phase Boundary

Phase 3 is complete when Phase 4 uses this atlas, taxonomy, workflow classification layer, and frequency matrix as its sole input. This summary intentionally does not classify opportunities, recommend solutions, or prioritize markets.
