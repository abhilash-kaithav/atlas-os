## Purpose

This folder records `RO-002`, a kill-oriented monopoly wedge discovery pass inside commercial trade-contractor accounts receivable.

The question was not whether subcontractor AR is painful.

The question was:

> Which single atomic workflow has the strongest practitioner-validated pain and the weakest incumbent answer?

## Outcome

The broad "commercial trade-contractor AR" surface does not survive as one company boundary.

One narrow wedge survives:

- Subcontractor-side pay-application rejection diagnosis and resubmission orchestration.

Everything else reviewed in this pass was killed because it was already being directly attacked by purpose-built tools, depended too heavily on GC-controlled systems, or was too broad to serve as a clean beachhead.
