# Top 5 Atomic Wedges

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Final ranking layer

## Monopoly Test

Each candidate must answer yes to all of these:

1. Buyer clearly identifiable?
2. Pain recurring?
3. ROI measurable?
4. One team can buy it?
5. Implementation without enterprise transformation?
6. Customers adopt before ERP change?
7. Can it expand into a broader platform later?

## Why Four Were Killed

### 2. Retainage release tracking and collection

- Pain is severe.
- Buyer is clear.
- But it is already a visible product surface for sub-AR tools and ERP add-ons.
- It is a stronger expansion module than a first monopoly wedge.

### 3. SOV normalization and billing-template mapping

- This is painful and repeated.
- But direct tools already market GC-specific custom pay apps and portal-aware billing.
- It is a strong capability inside a winning product, but not the cleanest first company boundary.

### 4. Change-order-to-SOV sync before billing

- Pain is real and money leaks here.
- But [Clearstory](https://www.g2.com/products/clearstory/reviews) and incumbent cost tools already directly attack this problem.
- It also depends more heavily on PM-owned upstream process than the winning wedge does.

### 5. Lien-waiver and notary exception orchestration

- Pain is obvious and annoying.
- But the workflow is already covered in part by GCPay, Procore Pay, Textura-class tools, and waiver-specific add-ons.
- The remaining gap is too entangled with payor-controlled systems and state-specific legal variation.

## Why The Winner Survived

The winning wedge is the one place where:

- the trade contractor feels monthly pain,
- the current stack still breaks into manual recovery,
- and there is no obvious category leader owning only that correction loop.
