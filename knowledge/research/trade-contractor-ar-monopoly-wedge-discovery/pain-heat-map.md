# Pain Heat Map

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Active ranking layer

## Scoring

Each atomic workflow was scored from `1` to `5` on:

- frequency,
- revenue impact,
- time consumed,
- emotional frustration,
- manual work,
- spreadsheet dependence,
- email dependence,
- phone dependence,
- number of parties involved.

The `Pain score` is the unweighted sum. The purpose is prioritization, not false precision.

## Readout

The ranking shows a clear pattern:

- The most painful steps are not initial ERP posting.
- The highest scores cluster around opaque exception handling after a packet is in motion.
- Retainage and collections are economically painful, but they are downstream and broader.
- The winner should come from the exception loop, not from generic reporting or basic billing creation.
