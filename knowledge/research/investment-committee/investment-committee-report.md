# Investment Committee Report

Last updated: 2026-08-16
Status: Complete

## Executive Summary

The committee started from rejection and only advanced candidates that still cleared a hard qualification review using the completed canonical discovery base. `W-001` and `W-002` passed because they combine workflow-complete understanding, repeated customer evidence, recurring pain, identifiable buyers, bottom-up economics, and a still-credible incumbent-boundary explanation. `W-003`, `W-004`, and `W-005` were rejected at qualification because the same borderline evidence and boundary concerns that kept them `YELLOW` in discovery were still unresolved. After ranking the surviving candidates across independent dimensions, stress-testing the results, and running an adversarial review, the committee recommends `W-001` as `Tier 1` and `W-002` as `Tier 2`. No company is selected.

| Candidate | Prior status | Committee decision | Why |

## Comparative Readout

- `W-001` won the committee on customer pain, urgency, buyer clarity, direct ROI, incumbent weakness, and defensibility.
- `W-002` won on product simplicity, implementation ease, capital efficiency, time to PMF, and expansion path.
- No surviving candidate was Pareto-dominated.
- Sensitivity testing showed `W-001` remained the most robust winner unless customer urgency and buyer clarity were explicitly de-emphasized.

## Final Recommendation

| Tier | Candidate | Recommendation | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | `W-001` | Strong recommendation for founder validation | It is the sharpest combination of pain, measurable economics, repeated evidence, and credible startup entry. |
| Tier 2 | `W-002` | Validate further before founder commitment | It is promising and likely easier to bring to PMF, but the budget and expansion thesis still matter more than in `W-001`. |
| Rejected | `W-003`, `W-004`, `W-005` | Do not actively pursue | All three still require evidence that should have existed before the committee phase. |

## Committee Interpretation

The committee did not find a hidden winner inside the `YELLOW` set. The post-discovery evaluation mostly confirmed what the calibrated discovery program already implied: the strongest remaining opportunities are not broad software categories, but narrow recovery loops with direct economic deadlines. The committee now prefers founder validation depth over further portfolio expansion.
