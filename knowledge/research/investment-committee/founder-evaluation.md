# Founder Evaluation

Last updated: 2026-08-16
Status: Complete with founder-data constraint

## Constraint

The canonical Atlas knowledge base contains no specific founder profile, distribution channel, or customer-access advantage.

Founder evaluation is therefore limited to how sensitive each surviving candidate is to founder-specific strengths or weaknesses.

## Candidate Readout

| Dimension | `W-001` | `W-002` | Committee interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domain credibility required | High | Medium | Construction billing recovery requires sharper domain trust. |
| Access to customers | Harder | Easier | Multifamily operations should be easier to access than subcontractor finance teams. |
| Learning curve | Steeper | Shallower | `W-001` requires more complex billing, waiver, and portal context. |
| Long-term motivation | Unknown | Unknown | No founder-specific data exists. |
| Execution capability required | High | Medium | `W-001` combines workflow logic, documents, and counterparty coordination. |
| Existing relationships | Unknown | Unknown | Not available in canonical inputs. |
| Unique advantage needed | Higher | Moderate | `W-001` benefits more from construction-specific trust and insight. |

## Committee Interpretation

- No candidate receives positive founder-fit credit because the committee does not have founder-specific evidence.
- `W-002` is more forgiving if founder access is shallow.
- `W-001` is still the stronger opportunity, but it is also the more domain-gated one.
- Founder fit cannot rescue any rejected candidate and does not override the `Tier 1` / `Tier 2` ordering.
