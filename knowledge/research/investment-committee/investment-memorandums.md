## Investment Memo — W-001

### Investment Thesis

Invest in founder validation around a neutral construction billing-recovery layer that diagnoses blocked pay apps, assembles missing support, and accelerates corrected resubmission across GC-controlled portals.

### Supporting Evidence

- High-confidence customer evidence from reviews, practitioner testimony, and role definitions
- Clear workflow-complete support in the canonical matrix and construction inventory
- Strong gate performance across discovery and committee qualification

### Customer Reality

The work is already staffed manually by project accountants and AR teams. Customers do not lack construction software; they lack a single owner for correction work once a billing record is rejected.

### Economics

The wedge combines direct labor savings with faster cash collection, lower financing stress, and less short-pay leakage.

### Competition

Large incumbents already control key systems, but they remain biased toward portal submission, ERP recordkeeping, or owner-side workflow control.

### Risks

- Bespoke exception logic may reduce repeatability
- Portals could improve blocker visibility
- Some buyers may still prefer labor over new software

### Unknowns

- Rejection-volume concentration
- Common blocker taxonomy depth
- Standalone budget willingness

### Validation Remaining

- Measure exception concentration by subcontractor profile
- Confirm price tolerance against current labor and financing pain
- Prove repeatable diagnosis patterns across counterparties

### Reasons To Invest

- Strongest evidence base in the current portfolio
- Sharpest buyer and ROI clarity
- Most robust result across committee stress tests

### Reasons To Pass

- Implementation and exception heterogeneity could be worse than expected
- Construction fintech incumbents are not asleep

### Recommended Next Validation

Run founder-validation interviews with subcontractor controllers and project accountants focused on rejected pay-app frequency, recovery burden, and willingness to pay for neutral multi-portal recovery.

## Investment Memo — W-002

### Investment Thesis

Validate a readiness-recovery layer for multifamily turns that identifies blockers early, coordinates vendors and approvals, and protects ready dates before vacancy days compound.

### Supporting Evidence

- Strong practitioner signals that maintenance coordination and vendor-response recovery still leave the PMS
- Clear human control-tower roles already exist
- Confirmed pattern adjacency through accommodation merge evidence

### Customer Reality

Customers do not lack maintenance tools. They lack clear ownership when a ready date slips and vendor, access, approval, and inspection dependencies have to be recovered in real time.

### Economics

Value comes from avoided vacancy days, better ready-date performance, fewer repeat trips, and less coordinator labor. The budget case is real, but less concentrated than `W-001`.

### Competition

PMS and maintenance incumbents are strong and embedded, which is why the thesis must stay tightly focused on readiness recovery rather than generic work-order management.

### Risks

- Missed ready dates may be driven by structural capex or labor shortages more than coordination failure
- PMS vendors can plausibly improve blocker visibility
- Operators may continue tolerating the problem with human coordinators

### Unknowns

- Willingness to pay for a dedicated readiness-recovery layer
- Software-addressable share of missed-ready-date pain
- True expansion path beyond multifamily

### Validation Remaining

- Separate coordination failures from structural delay causes
- Validate budget ownership with regional operators and asset managers
- Test whether accommodation-style expansion is real or merely analogous

### Reasons To Invest

- Strong operational pain and recurring workflow
- Simpler product and lower-risk path to PMF
- Better long-term expansion case than the current leader

### Reasons To Pass

- Weaker buyer clarity and defensibility than `W-001`
- Higher risk that incumbents absorb the missing feature set

### Recommended Next Validation

Run founder-validation interviews with regional operations leaders and maintenance coordinators focused on missed-ready-date root causes, current workaround cost, and budget willingness for a dedicated recovery layer.
