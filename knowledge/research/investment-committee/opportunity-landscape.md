# Opportunity Landscape

Last updated: 2026-08-16
Status: Complete

## Buyer Types

- Finance-control buyers tied to delayed cash or closing risk: strongest in `W-001`
- Operations buyers tied to revenue-ready dates: strongest in `W-002`
- Rejected candidates often had real end users but weaker software budget ownership

## Workflow Patterns

- The surviving opportunities remain concentrated in recovery, reconciliation, and correction loops
- Happy-path systems of record still leave value behind when something misses a time boundary
- The committee saw no reason to reopen a horizontal platform thesis

## Recovery Loops

- `W-001`: blocked submission -> diagnosis -> evidence assembly -> correction -> resubmission
- `W-002`: planned ready date -> blocker -> recovery coordination -> resequencing -> ready-state restoration
- Rejected `YELLOW` candidates followed similar mechanics, but did not clear evidence or boundary thresholds strongly enough

## Economic Patterns

- The strongest surviving economics tie directly to either delayed cash or delayed revenue readiness
- Labor savings matter, but they are not sufficient by themselves
- The committee favored workflows where labor savings stack with deadline-sensitive economic loss

## Incumbent Failures

- Incumbents still own the record more reliably than the recovery loop
- The clean-path workflow is usually better served than the diagnosis-and-correction desk
- Rejected candidates were not rejected because pain was absent; they were rejected because the committee was not convinced the remaining gap was startup-sized

## AI Opportunities

- AI is most useful where documents, distributed context, and resubmission logic are central
- AI is less compelling when the root cause is budget scarcity, staffing shortages, or incumbent bundle power
- The committee still prefers operational ownership gaps over AI novelty

## Expansion and Platform Opportunities

- `W-002` has the stronger cross-workflow expansion path
- `W-001` has the stronger first wedge
- The committee did not promote any cross-industry pattern into a platform thesis

## Cross-Industry Themes

- Atlas continues to find the best opportunities after the happy path breaks
- Borderline evidence is not enough after discovery; the committee removes candidates that still need proof discovery should already have produced
- The portfolio now looks more like a founder-validation queue than a broad market map
