# OV-01 Kill Sheet

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Surviving Phase 4 opportunity

## Thesis

Build a cross-system decision-memory layer that converts calls, emails, attachments, portal activity, and side notes into structured rationale, obligations, and next-action context linked back to operational records.

## Evidence Base

- Anchored structural failure: `SF-03` Decision Context Escapes the Record
- Atlas coverage: 174 workflows, 219 usage links, 50 industries, $45.42T of 2025 gross-output surface
- Supporting failure overlap: Human Judgment Under Incomplete Information (92); Handoff and Approval Latency (38); Cross-System Reconciliation (42)
- Example workflows: Billing, Claims, and Collections (Care Delivery and Reimbursement); Clinical Documentation (Care Delivery and Reimbursement); Access, Admissions, and Throughput Management (Care Delivery and Reimbursement)

## Why Customers May Not Buy

Security, privacy, and internal politics may block access to email, messages, and call notes. Some teams may also view pervasive context capture as surveillance rather than enablement.

## Why Incumbents May Still Win

Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, or category leaders could bundle context capture into suites customers already trust if the startup does not establish superior workflow writeback and domain specificity quickly.

## Why The Market May Be Too Early Or Too Late

Too early if buyers accept note-taking demos but will not operationalize writeback. Too late if suite vendors commoditize summarization before a startup owns the system-of-action layer.

## Critical Assumptions

Customers will authorize access to the unstructured channels that contain real state, and the product will measurably improve turn times, denial rates, rework, or accountability.

## Evidence That Would Invalidate The Thesis

Pilots show that teams only want passive summaries, not operational writeback; adoption stalls because legal or security policy blocks access to decisive context; or incumbents win simply by adding transcript summarization.

## Timing Validation

This thesis becomes more credible now because modern AI can interpret documents and messages at operational quality, while regulated workflows increasingly require traceable digital state instead of informal handoffs.
