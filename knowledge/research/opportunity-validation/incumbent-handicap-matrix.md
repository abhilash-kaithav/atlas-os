# Incumbent Handicap Matrix

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Active Phase 4 matrix

| ID | Candidate | Handicap types | Why incumbents have not solved it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OV-01 | Decision-Memory Infrastructure | Technical debt, Installed base, Switching costs, Organizational inertia | ERP, CRM, EHR, and service platforms optimize for structured fields and transactions, while collaboration suites optimize for conversation. Neither side is architected to own the full decision chain or write a reliable narrative back into shared workflow state. |
| OV-02 | Exception-Resolution System of Action | Business-model conflict, Technical debt, Installed base, Services revenue dependence | Core vendors benefit from stable transaction processing and often rely on partners or services teams for exception-heavy work. They are poorly positioned to unify the messy long tail that cuts across systems, policies, and teams. |
| OV-03 | Reconciliation Truth Layer | Technical debt, Installed base, Regulatory exposure, Services revenue dependence | Existing financial platforms and close tools are optimized for their own domain models. They still struggle when identifiers, documents, counterparties, and timing differ across systems. |
| OV-04 | Live Replanning and Recovery Control | Technical debt, Installed base, Channel conflict, Organizational inertia | Planning suites are sold as forecast and optimization systems, not as minute-by-minute operational recovery layers. Their implementations are slow, and their models depend on cleaner inputs than real operations provide. |
| OV-05 | Compliance Evidence Graph | Regulatory exposure, Technical debt, Services revenue dependence, Organizational inertia | GRC, quality, and reporting tools often own the checklist or the filing but not the live operational evidence chain. Much of the painful work is still delegated to manual attestations and spreadsheets. |
| RJ-01 | Generic Judgment Workbench | Weak standalone handicap | Every incumbent suite can add an AI assistant. The startup has no obvious structural reason incumbents cannot copy the surface area. |
| RJ-02 | Approval and Onboarding Coordination Layer | Crowded category, Weak timing shift | BPM, CRM, service management, ticketing, and onboarding software already attack this surface area. |
| RJ-03 | Multi-Party Trust Network | Network effects, Adoption friction | Incumbents are constrained, but that does not automatically mean a startup can win first. It may just mean the problem requires ecosystem power. |
