# Top 3 Wedges

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Final ranking layer

## Ranking

| Rank | Wedge | Why it made the Top 3 | Why it did not automatically win | Final decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Trade-contractor pay-app, lien-waiver, and change-order exception resolution | Clear buyer, direct cash-flow ROI, persistent spreadsheet and portal pain, wrong-side incumbent orientation | Still faces GCPay, Textura, Procore, Autodesk, and Siteline; requires proof that trade contractors will buy a dedicated workflow tool at scale | GREEN |
| 2 | Provider denials, appeals, and recoupments | Huge measurable pain, clear buyer, repeatable exception work | Waystar, Notable, SmarterDx, and existing RCM leaders are already active; stress test fails | KILL |
| 3 | B2B SaaS usage-billing and entitlement exceptions | Strong public complaint density, clear finance buyer, modern billing complexity | Mature field with Zuora, Chargebee, Maxio, Stripe Billing, Salesforce Revenue Management, and Metronome | KILL |

## Why Construction Won

Construction won because it was the only wedge where the evidence showed all of the following at once:

1. customers still run material pieces of the workflow in spreadsheets or side systems,
2. current category tools are strong but not truly centered on the trade-contractor buyer,
3. the buyer feels direct cash-flow pain and can justify spend.

## Why The Other Two Lost

- Healthcare lost because the market already noticed the pain and built against it.
- SaaS monetization lost because the pain is real but the competitive field is already too deep.
