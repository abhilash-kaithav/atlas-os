# Final Recommendation

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Final recommendation

## Recommendation

Kill Exception Resolution as a horizontal company thesis.

Preserve exactly one narrow follow-on wedge:

> commercial trade-contractor pay-application, lien-waiver, and change-order exception resolution

## Why

- Customers still experience repeated exception pain after paying for software.
- The pain is concentrated in one side-specific workflow, not in a reusable horizontal platform.
- The subcontractor-side buyer has direct, measurable exposure to payment delay and billing rejection.
- Current leaders mostly optimize for the GC or owner side, leaving a narrower trade-contractor workflow opening.

## What Not To Do

- Do not reopen a generic Exception Resolution platform thesis.
- Do not treat healthcare denials or SaaS monetization exceptions as underowned markets.
- Do not assume AI by itself creates wedge defensibility.

## Next Step

Run direct buyer validation on the surviving construction wedge:

1. interview trade-contractor controllers and AR leaders,
2. measure monthly pay-app rework, waiver burden, and aging by project,
3. map the most common GC portal combinations,
4. test whether buyers want a neutral cross-portal workflow tool or only incremental automation inside existing portals.

If those buyer checks fail, kill Exception Resolution entirely.
