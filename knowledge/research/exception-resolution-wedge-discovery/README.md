# Exception Resolution Wedge Discovery

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Active research objective layer

## Purpose

This folder records `RO-001`, a kill-oriented wedge discovery pass for the broad Exception Resolution opportunity.

The question was not whether the old thesis sounded attractive.

The question was:

> Where do customers still experience costly exception handling even after they already pay for software?

## Outcome

The broad Exception Resolution thesis does not survive.

One narrow wedge survives the research pass:

- Trade-contractor pay-application, lien-waiver, and change-order exception resolution in commercial construction.

All other wedges reviewed in this pass were killed because they were already occupied by strong category vendors, had weak buyer separation from current systems, or lacked a convincing answer to the category-leader stress test.

- Do not reopen the broad horizontal category unless new evidence materially changes the competitive map.
