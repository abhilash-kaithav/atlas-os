# RO-001 Executive Summary

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Approved research summary

## Headline

Exception Resolution fails as a horizontal company thesis.

After mapping 20 exception-heavy workflows, reviewing current vendors, and prioritizing customer-reported pain over marketing claims, only one narrow wedge remains credible:

> trade-contractor pay-application, lien-waiver, and change-order exception resolution

## What Survived

### Recommended wedge

- Wedge: commercial construction subcontractor billing exceptions
- Core workflow: `Progress Billing and Compliance Administration`
- Buyer: controller, AR lead, or CFO at a trade contractor
- Why it survives:
  - Current systems are mostly optimized for the GC or owner side, not the subcontractor AR side.
  - Customer evidence still shows spreadsheets, external logs, manual waiver handling, manual schedule-of-values work, and project-by-project portal friction.
  - The buyer has direct cash-flow pain and a reason to purchase even when upstream counterparties force use of GCPay, Textura, or Procore.

## What Failed

### Broad category

The horizontal "Exception Resolution platform" thesis is killed.

It breaks for the same reason Phase 4B killed the broader opportunity:

- where pain is greatest, category leaders are already active,
- where pain is still visible, it often sits inside a very specific workflow,
- the startup boundary only becomes believable after narrowing to a side-specific, workflow-specific exception surface.

### Strongest killed wedges

1. Provider-side denials and recoupments
   - Very large pain.
   - Very strong buyer.
   - Already heavily occupied by Waystar, Notable, SmarterDx, AKASA-class automation, and existing RCM stacks.
2. B2B SaaS usage-billing and contract exceptions
   - Real customer pain around credits, one-off invoicing, entitlement edge cases, and reporting.
   - Already crowded with Zuora, Chargebee, Maxio, Stripe Billing, Salesforce Revenue Management, and Metronome-class entrants.
3. Freight invoice and settlement exceptions
   - Real pain around invoice capture, POD quality, disputes, and accessorials.
   - Already occupied by freight-audit leaders plus TMS and visibility platforms.

## Final Recommendation

Keep exploring Exception Resolution only through the construction subcontractor billing wedge.

Kill the broader category.

The next step should not be generic thesis expansion. It should be direct buyer validation on the surviving construction wedge.
