# Worker Role Scan

Last updated: 2026-08-16
Status: Canonical role-scan layer

## Purpose

This scan looks for roles centered on investigating, reconciling, validating, coordinating, correcting, escalating, following up, and resubmitting. These roles often reveal where software still has not fully absorbed the work.

## Program Interpretation

- The preserved wedges correspond to workflows that companies already staff with dedicated exception owners.
- The strongest surviving roles sit at the boundary between system-of-record automation and unresolved cross-party recovery work.
- Role scan evidence is supportive, not sufficient by itself; it strengthens preserved wedges but does not override weak customer evidence or crowded boundaries.
