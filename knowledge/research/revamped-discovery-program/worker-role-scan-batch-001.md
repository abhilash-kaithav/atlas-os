# Worker Role Scan

Last updated: 2026-08-15
Status: Batch 1 role-scan complete

## Purpose

This scan looks for roles centered on investigating, reconciling, validating, coordinating, correcting, escalating, following up, and resubmitting. These roles often reveal where software still has not fully absorbed the work.

## Batch 1 Interpretation

- The construction, housing, and staffing role scans reinforce that the pain sits in ongoing exception ownership, not just in a one-time implementation gap.
- The restaurant role scan confirms real work, but it points more toward tolerated bookkeeping labor than toward a clearly defensible new software wedge.
