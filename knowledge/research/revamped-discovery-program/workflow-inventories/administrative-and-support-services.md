# Administrative and Support Services Workflow Inventory

Last updated: 2026-08-15
Status: Batch 1 inventory complete

## Industry Context

- Industry: `Administrative and support services`
- Operating system: `Workforce Coordination and Service Operations`
- Lifecycle: client sales -> scope and rate setup -> recruiting -> screening and compliance onboarding -> assignment setup -> scheduling -> time capture -> payroll and billing -> collections -> service review -> renewal, replacement, or offboarding

## Workflow Inventory

| Lifecycle stage | Workflow | Description | Primary roles | External parties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial | Client sales, scoping, and rate setup | Define bill rates, service scope, margin expectations, and account rules. | account executive; implementation lead | client; VMS admin |
| Commercial / Partner | Client and VMS onboarding | Configure account hierarchy, timesheet rules, approvers, portals, and integrations. | implementations analyst; middle-office admin | client AP; MSP; VMS provider |
| Workforce | Recruiting and candidate submission | Source workers, qualify fit, and submit to client or VMS channels. | recruiter; account manager | candidates; client; MSP |
| Workforce / Risk | Credentialing and compliance onboarding | Collect I-9, licenses, onboarding packets, policies, and client-specific documents. | onboarding coordinator; compliance specialist | candidates; clients; screening vendors |
| Workforce | Assignment setup, scheduling, and worker communication | Activate placements, track shift rules, and communicate schedule or assignment changes. | scheduler; recruiter; account manager | candidates; site supervisors |
| Workforce / Financial | Time capture and approval | Collect weekly time, resolve did-not-work or open disputes, and secure client approval. | timecard admin; payroll coordinator | candidates; client approvers; VMS |
| Recovery | Pay/bill discrepancy resolution | Diagnose mismatches across BTE, ATS, VMS, rates, approvals, and pay/bill rules before payroll or invoicing closes. | billing operations coordinator; reconciliation analyst | client AP; VMS; candidates |
| Financial | Payroll processing | Finalize pay transactions, exports, statutory rules, and issue correction runs when needed. | payroll specialist; payroll manager | employees; payroll providers |
| Financial | Client invoicing, cash application, and collections | Convert approved hours to invoices, apply receipts, and chase underpayments or disputes. | billing specialist; AR specialist | clients; MSPs; lockbox providers |
| Governance / Customer | Service-level monitoring, reporting, and escalation | Track fill rates, worker attendance, SLA issues, and client escalations. | operations manager; account manager | clients; site supervisors |
| Closeout | Renewal, replacement, and offboarding | Close placements, backfill openings, resolve final time, and capture account changes. | recruiter; operations lead | clients; candidates |
