## Reopen Rule

Reopen a killed Batch 1 candidate only if contradictory evidence shows:

- repeated independent practitioner demand for a standalone product,
- clear measurable ROI not already captured by incumbent suites,
- and a structural reason large category owners still would not dominate the wedge.
