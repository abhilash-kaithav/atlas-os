## Interpretation

- Every candidate now has current direct vendors, not just adjacent software.
- Several candidates also show a second warning sign: the leading vendor already speaks in the same language Atlas used in Phase 4, such as `system of record`, `execution layer`, `decision memory`, `autonomous resolution`, or `continuous evidence`.
- That does not mean the markets are closed forever. It means Atlas did not find underowned category surfaces strong enough to advance as venture theses today.
