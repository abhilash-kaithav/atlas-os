## Stress Test Commentary

### OV-01

If Microsoft, ServiceNow, Salesforce, OpenAI, and the current direct category vendors all prioritized this wedge tomorrow, Atlas does not yet show why they would fail.

The platforms already own the context surfaces.

### OV-02

If the leading category vendor fully commits, it already has the distribution edge that matters: payer connectivity, forms, appeals workflows, and claims context.

That makes the wedge attractive but not defensible enough.

### OV-03

This market rewards historical data, operator trust, and explainable controls.

Those are precisely the assets current leaders already have.

### OV-04

This market rewards live network data and operational embedding.

That advantage currently sits with the vendors already building control towers, execution graphs, and recovery agents.

### OV-05

This market rewards regulatory fit, proven audit workflows, and vertical referenceability.

Atlas currently does not show a founder-specific advantage strong enough to outrun existing niche leaders.

## Conclusion

Phase 4 overused "startup advantage" as a proxy for founder advantage.

Phase 4B applies the stricter test:

> Could this repo's future founder plausibly win the first wedge before current category leaders, platform incumbents, or vertical specialists do?

For all five candidates, the answer is currently no.
