# Phase 4B Executive Summary

Last updated: 2026-08-14
Status: Approved Phase 4B summary

## Headline

Phase 4B did not validate a single Phase 4 survivor strongly enough to earn Phase 5.

Every one of the five surviving territories from Phase 4 points to a real pain surface. None of them currently demonstrates a sufficiently defensible, evidence-backed underserved wedge once the current market landscape is examined directly.

## Final Decisions

| ID | Candidate | Decision | Why it was killed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OV-01 | Decision-Memory Infrastructure | KILL | The horizontal thesis is already occupied by direct category vendors such as Aera and Cloverpop, while Microsoft, ServiceNow, and workflow-native vertical products already control the distribution points the thesis needs. |
| OV-02 | Exception-Resolution System of Action | KILL | The pain is severe, but the strongest wedges are already being productized by domain leaders such as Waystar and ServiceNow. The audit found visible ROI, not a defensible whitespace. |
| OV-03 | Reconciliation Truth Layer | KILL | Finance reconciliation, intercompany, freight audit, and unstructured matching already support multiple direct vendors with AI and explainability claims. The thesis describes an active market, not an ignored one. |
| OV-04 | Live Replanning and Recovery Control | KILL | Real-time recovery is strategically important, but control-tower, planning, logistics-visibility, and field-operations vendors are already moving directly into this layer. The remaining gap is too fragmented to call venture whitespace. |
| OV-05 | Compliance Evidence Graph | KILL | Horizontal compliance evidence collection is crowded and the strongest vertical wedges already have purpose-built vendors in life sciences, DSCSA, healthcare oversight, and trust management. |

## What Phase 4B Actually Proved

Phase 4 was directionally useful but too optimistic.

Its five survivors were best understood as market hypotheses, not venture theses.

Phase 4B shows that:

1. Recurring structural failure does not imply category vacancy.
2. Strong buyer pain does not imply defensible startup entry.
3. "Founder advantage" must be replaced by "earned wedge access plus structural defensibility."
4. The competitive stress test against category leaders needs to happen before ranking, not after.

## What Atlas Should Do Next

Do not start Phase 5 from the current five.

Instead:

1. Preserve the five survivors as failed but instructive hypotheses.
2. Make a market-reality mini-audit mandatory before any future opportunity is ranked as a survivor.
3. Reopen candidate formation only where Atlas can show all three of the following:
   - a narrow workflow wedge,
   - a visible budget owner with direct ROI,
   - no already-entrenched category leader solving the same wedge in production.

## Recommendation

Phase 4B should be marked complete and approved.

Phase 5 should remain not started because Atlas currently has zero evidence-backed GREEN opportunities.
